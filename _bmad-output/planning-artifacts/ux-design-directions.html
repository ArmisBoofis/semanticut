How to choose (quick rubric)

  • Reviewer / POC clarity: Direction 1 or 7.
  • Long demos / dark rooms: Direction 2.
  • Future transcript/snippet column: Direction 3.
  • Lots of status on screen: Direction 4.
  • Softer brand, still professional: Direction 5 or 6.
  • Thumb-first / narrow: Direction 8.
